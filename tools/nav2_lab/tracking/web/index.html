Shared physics and controller settings

Same limits for both controllers
Physics 1 ms · controller 25 sim-Hz · wheel loop 100 sim-Hz0.25×

At 0.25 RTF the 25 sim-Hz Nav2 controller runs at roughly 6.25 wall-Hz and the 100 sim-Hz wheel loop at 25 wall-Hz. Controller frequencies are NOT divided by four.

SMC gains

RPP starts with the published production RPP parameters. SMC uses a continuous saturation boundary layer to limit switching chatter. Both controllers are limited to |v| + 0.21|ω| ≤ 0.50 m/s.

Physics provenance

Physics is loaded from saved Mobility and Odometry Lab YAMLs, falling back to explicitly published calibration where necessary. The floor has no cameras, obstacles or light arrays.
