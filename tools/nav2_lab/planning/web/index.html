Map and perception

Static map

Generated from the existing badminton_court.sdf and court_landmarks.yaml. Only net poles and AprilTag mount plates are occupied; the net and court lines are not.

Depth observations

Simulated RealSense → point cloud → filtered depth scan → Nav2 obstacle layer. Boxes and walking people are not inserted directly into the static map.

Comparison rule

Keep obstacles stationary when comparing all planners. A moving person can change the observed costmap between sequential requests.

Changing map resolution or depth-layer setting requires restarting the lab simulation. This lab deliberately spawns the robot at world (0,0,0), so the court map aligns with odom.

What should appear in RViz?

The static map shows poles and tag mounts immediately. A box or human appears in the dynamic costmap only after it enters the depth camera's field of view. Moving it out of view may eventually clear it.

Before benchmarking a dynamic obstacle, check that it is visible in the depth scan and global costmap in RViz. No ground-truth obstacle positions are fed to the Nav2 costmap.
