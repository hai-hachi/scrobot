Start simulation to measure topic rates.

Wall Hz is measured against monotonic wall time. Sim Hz divides message arrivals by the /clock progression in the same window. Image and PointCloud2 payloads are received as serialized messages; the monitor reads only their CDR header timestamps, not their pixel or point data. Header interval p95 and jitter come from source stamps, while sim Hz still comes from /clock progression. Unique gap totals count intervals over 1.5× nominal since observer startup; they are not necessarily dropped frames or controller deadline misses. Costmap full and update streams are non-periodic: an initial latched map can be valid without further updates. Clock minus header stamp is not one-way transport latency; slightly negative values can occur because of callback ordering.

Correct interpretation

At 0.5× RTF, a correctly synchronized 30-Hz simulated depth camera normally produces about 15 messages/second in wall time. A 100-Hz simulated ROS timer normally fires about 50 times per wall second. We flag unexpectedly low/high simulated-time cadence separately from a valid wall-clock slowdown.

Nav2 controller warning: A log line saying the 25 Hz controller missed its rate while showing a higher "current loop rate" is possible. The reported figure is calculated from computation time for that cycle; the warning comes from the rate scheduler missing its wake-up deadline. Do not lower the configured controller Hz until sim-time cadence, costmap timing, TF availability and scheduler behavior are checked.

Important limitation: shared /clock does not guarantee lockstep between Gazebo and callbacks. We still have to inspect queueing, timestamp age, simulated timeouts and controller deadline warnings. This initial lab measures synchronization; it does not implement an acknowledgment-based global stepping scheduler.
